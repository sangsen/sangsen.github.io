Privacy Policy

Gold Bill Calculator

Effective date: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gold Bill Calculator ("the app") handles information when you use it. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Gold Bill Calculator is designed to work without requiring an account or personal sign-up. Values you enter — such as gold rate, weight, making charges, discounts, and old-gold exchange details — are used only to perform calculations and are not transmitted to us.

We do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifiers through the app's calculator functionality.

Advertising

The app is provided free of charge and is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect and use device identifiers, approximate location, and other information to serve and measure advertisements, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier through your device's settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Aside from advertising data handled by Google AdMob as described above, we do not share calculation data with third parties, because that data is not transmitted to us in the first place.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose gold calculation and estimation tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention

Values you enter into the calculator remain on your device and are cleared or overwritten as you use the app. We do not maintain a server-side record of your calculations.

Your Choices

You may stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it. You can also manage ad personalization and device permissions through your device's operating system settings.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make a material change, we will update the effective date shown at the top of this policy.

We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@sangsen.space .
